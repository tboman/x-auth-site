Brand Guidelines

The identity system for X-Auth and its product lines — Verify, Risk, and Orchestration. This document defines the Cipher mark, its lockups, color, typography, and the rules that keep them consistent everywhere they appear.

01

The mark

Cipher

Five equal facets arranged in a quincunx resolve into an X. Nothing in the mark is literal — no padlock, no shield — but the idea is precise: independent signals, read together, produce a verdict. It is how X-Auth works, drawn in geometry.

The construction is five identical squares rotated 45°, set on a square grid. The flat edges of neighboring facets face each other, cutting four clean channels through the mark. It stays legible from a 16-pixel favicon to a building sign.

GEOMETRY Five squares of equal size, rotated 45°, centers on a 2×2 grid plus its midpoint.
PROPORTION Facet size to grid spacing is fixed at 1 : 2. Never redraw, re-space, or scale facets independently.
ORIENTATION The mark sits on its points — always. It is never rotated to sit on a flat edge.
02

Lockups

The wordmark is set in Space Grotesk Bold, lowercase, with −2% letter-spacing. The mark’s height matches the wordmark’s cap-to-baseline height plus its descender. The horizontal lockup is the default; the stacked version is for centered, square, or narrow placements.

Clearspace & minimum size

The minimum clear zone equals the height of one facet (one third of the mark). Nothing enters it — text, rules, page edges, other logos.

07

Misuse

Don’t rotate the mark off its points.
Don’t recolor — especially not status colors.
Don’t outline the facets.
Don’t add shadows, gradients, or effects.
Approved colorways Navy on light, white on Ink or Navy, single-color black for print. Nothing else.
Wordmark integrity Always lowercase, always Space Grotesk Bold, never re-spaced, never typeset by hand.
Backgrounds Solid Ink, Navy, Paper, or white only. Never over photography or patterned fills.
