## 2. The "Triple Threat" (Core Features)
**Goal:** Breakdown the three pillars of the service with a focus on "active" protection.

### A. Adaptive Authentication
*   **Headline:** Authentication That Never Sleeps.
*   **Copy:** Beyond passwords. Implement biometrics, MFA, and magic links that adapt to user behavior. One line of code for a lifetime of security.
*   **Keywords:** Passwordless, Biometric, WebAuthn, FIDO2.

### B. Scalable Authorization
*   **Headline:** Permissions Without the Headache.
*   **Copy:** Manage complex RBAC and ABAC at scale. Our edge-computed authorization engine ensures latency-free policy enforcement across your entire stack.
*   **Keywords:** RBAC, ABAC, Policy-as-Code, Global Edge.

### C. Predictive Risk Services
*   **Headline:** Know the Risk, Before the Request.
*   **Copy:** Stop account takeovers (ATO) and bot attacks in their tracks. Our engine analyzes device fingerprints, IP reputation, and behavioral anomalies in real-time.
*   **Keywords:** Anomaly Detection, Bot Mitigation, Risk Scoring, Geo-fencing.
